🎲 Pig Game - Guide

🌟 About This Project

Pig Game is a classic two-player dice game where players race to reach 100 points. It's a game of chance versus strategy - will you keep rolling to build your score, or play it safe and hold? Built with modern web technologies for a smooth gaming experience!

🎮 How to Play

  • 🎯 Objective: Be the first player to reach 100 points
  • 🎲 Roll Dice: Click "Roll Dice" to get a random number between 1 and 6
  • ➕ Add Points: Your roll is added to your "current" temporary score
  • ✋ Hold: Click "Hold" to add your current score to your total score and pass to next player
  • ⚠️ Rolling a 1: If you roll a 1, you lose all current points and it's the next player's turn
  • 🏆 Winning: First player to reach 100 or more points wins!

💡 Strategy Tips

  • Know when to hold: Don't get greedy! Consider holding after reaching 20-25 current points
  • If you're ahead, play safer. If behind, take more risks
  • Watch your opponent's score and adjust your strategy accordingly
  • Remember: rolling a 1 loses everything, so balance risk vs reward

⚙️ Technical Features

  • Built with vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks)
  • Responsive design for desktop and mobile
  • Smooth animations and visual feedback
  • Accessible with ARIA labels and keyboard support
  • Clean UI with player turn indicators
